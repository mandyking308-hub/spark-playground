# Aurelia World — Data Processing Addendum (DPA) template

**Operator / supplier:** Global Solutions Management LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("GSM")  
**Product:** Aurelia World  
**Status:** Counsel-review draft — 22 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Template for schools, education groups, sponsors and organisations. This document must be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel before being used as a signed contractual DPA.

---

## 1. Scope and precedence

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between GSM and the customer identified in the applicable order form or master services agreement (the "Customer") where GSM processes personal data on the Customer's behalf in connection with Aurelia World.

If there is a conflict between this DPA and the general Terms of Use on a matter concerning Customer Personal Data processed by GSM as processor/service provider, this DPA controls. Any applicable standard contractual clauses or legally required transfer addendum controls over inconsistent terms in this DPA for the relevant transfer.

## 2. Definitions

- **Applicable Data Protection Law** means privacy, data-protection and student-privacy law applicable to the relevant processing, which may include UK GDPR, EU GDPR, applicable US state privacy law, FERPA, COPPA and other education/privacy law as applicable.
- **Customer Personal Data** means personal data processed by GSM solely on behalf of Customer under documented instructions.
- **Child Data** means Customer Personal Data relating to a person treated as a child or minor under applicable law.
- **Subprocessor** means a third party engaged by GSM to process Customer Personal Data on GSM's behalf.
- **Security Incident** means a confirmed breach of security leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Customer Personal Data.

## 3. Controller/processor role split

The parties acknowledge that role depends on the processing purpose.

### 3.1 GSM as processor/service provider

For education records, school roster information, teacher-entered verification data and other information the Customer directs GSM to process solely to provide the contracted institutional service, Customer is controller/business and GSM is processor/service provider, to the extent those terms apply.

GSM will process such Customer Personal Data only:

1. on Customer's documented instructions;
2. to provide, secure and support the contracted Aurelia World service;
3. as required by law, after giving notice where legally permitted; or
4. as otherwise expressly agreed in writing.

### 3.2 GSM as independent controller

GSM may act as independent controller for processing it determines is reasonably necessary for:

- account and identity security;
- fraud, abuse and cyber-security prevention;
- child safeguarding and platform-integrity investigations;
- legal and regulatory compliance;
- billing, accounting and contract administration;
- enforcing platform-wide safety and acceptable-use rules; and
- maintaining evidence reasonably required for legal claims or regulatory duties.

This DPA does not convert independent-controller processing into processor processing.

## 4. Processing details

### Subject matter

Provision of Aurelia World's verified creative, learning, achievement, permission, community, safeguarding and institutional-administration services.

### Duration

For the term of the applicable agreement plus any limited return/deletion, backup, safeguarding, financial or legally required retention period.

### Nature and purposes

- provision and administration of authorised user accounts;
- school/group membership and role administration;
- provision of creative and learning workflows;
- teacher/school verification and Achievement Passport workflows;
- permission and approval workflows;
- support, security and service reliability;
- Customer-requested reporting and administration; and
- deletion/export/rights assistance.

### Data subjects

- children/students;
- parents and guardians;
- teachers and school staff;
- education-group administrators;
- organisation/sponsor staff;
- authorised adult alumni where included in the institutional service.

### Categories of data

Depending on Customer configuration:

- name and account identifiers;
- age band and country/jurisdiction;
- school, group, class or programme relationship;
- guardian relationship/permission records;
- project metadata, work intentionally shared with the institution, submissions and feedback;
- achievement/verification records;
- support and administration data;
- technical/security metadata.

**Protected design rule:** private child drafts are not converted into institution-accessible records merely because an institution sponsors the account. Media upload remains disabled until the product has a real protected quarantine/scanning pathway.

### Special-category/highly sensitive data

The institutional service is not intended as a general repository for health, biometric, counselling or other highly sensitive records. Customer must not instruct GSM to process such data unless the feature is approved for that purpose and the parties have confirmed a lawful basis and safeguards. Safeguarding records are separately restricted and may be processed by GSM as controller where necessary to protect a child or comply with law.

## 5. GSM processor obligations

Where GSM acts as processor/service provider, GSM will:

1. process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions, unless law requires otherwise;
2. ensure personnel authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to confidentiality obligations;
3. maintain technical and organisational security measures appropriate to risk;
4. assist Customer, taking into account the nature of processing, with data-subject requests where reasonably possible;
5. assist Customer with security, breach, DPIA and regulatory-consultation obligations to the extent required by applicable law and relevant to GSM's processing;
6. keep records required of processors/service providers;
7. on termination, delete or return Customer Personal Data as agreed, unless law requires retention;
8. make information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with processor obligations available to Customer, subject to confidentiality, security and proportionality safeguards; and
9. not sell Customer Personal Data or use institution-provided Child Data for behavioural advertising or unrelated commercial profiling.

## 6. Customer obligations

Customer will:

1. provide lawful, fair and documented instructions;
2. ensure it has a lawful basis and authority to disclose Customer Personal Data to GSM;
3. provide legally required notices to students, parents, staff and other data subjects;
4. obtain consent/authorisation where applicable;
5. configure and administer authorised-user access appropriately;
6. avoid instructing GSM to collect data not reasonably necessary for the contracted service;
7. promptly remove access for staff or users who no longer require it; and
8. notify GSM of jurisdiction-specific requirements that Customer requires GSM to satisfy under the agreement.

Nothing in this section transfers to Customer an obligation that law places directly on GSM.

## 7. Children and education-specific safeguards

GSM will not use institution-provided Child Data for targeted behavioural advertising, data brokerage, or unrelated commercial profiling.

Where United States COPPA permits a school to act as the parent's agent for education-only processing, GSM may rely on appropriately verified school authorisation only for the educational context and not for unrelated commercial uses. GSM remains responsible for its own COPPA obligations as operator and will provide required notices to the school/customer.

Where FERPA applies and Customer relies on the school-official exception, the parties intend that GSM:

- performs an institutional service/function for Customer;
- is under Customer's direct control regarding the use and maintenance of FERPA-protected education records to the extent required by law;
- uses those records only for the purpose for which disclosure was made; and
- does not redisclose FERPA-protected information except as authorised by Customer or permitted by law.

Customer remains responsible for determining whether a particular FERPA exception or consent basis applies to its disclosure.

## 8. Security measures

GSM's current baseline controls include, as applicable to the service:

- verified identities and role-based access;
- database row-level security and least-privilege design;
- server-side secrets and separation of privileged credentials from browser code;
- audit logging for sensitive actions;
- invitation-gated child onboarding;
- protected guardian permission workflows;
- separation between under-16 and 16+ environments;
- no open child directory or unrestricted adult-to-child messaging;
- private-by-default child projects;
- safety/moderation gates before wider publication;
- media upload disabled until protected scanning/quarantine is available;
- incident-response processes; and
- encryption and security controls supplied by contracted infrastructure providers.

GSM may update controls where the update does not materially reduce the overall protection of Customer Personal Data.

## 9. Security Incident notification

GSM will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data for which GSM acts as processor, and will provide information reasonably available to GSM that Customer requires to meet applicable notification duties.

Notification is not an admission of fault or legal liability.

## 10. Subprocessors

Customer gives GSM general written authorisation to use Subprocessors needed to provide the service, subject to Applicable Data Protection Law.

GSM will:

- impose data-protection obligations on Subprocessors appropriate to their processing;
- remain responsible for Subprocessor performance to the extent required by applicable law and the agreement; and
- maintain a current Subprocessor list or other reasonable notification mechanism before broad institutional launch.

**Counsel launch item:** approve the production Subprocessor list and change-notification/objection procedure before institutional contracts are signed.

## 11. International transfers

Where Customer Personal Data is transferred across borders and law requires a transfer mechanism, the parties will use the applicable lawful mechanism, which may include adequacy arrangements, approved standard contractual clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum/Agreement, or another recognised mechanism.

**Counsel launch item:** determine which modules are required for UK/EEA customers and complete the transfer schedule based on actual production vendors and data locations.

## 12. Data-subject and regulator assistance

If GSM receives a request concerning Customer Personal Data for which Customer is controller, GSM may direct the requester to Customer unless law requires GSM to respond directly. GSM will provide reasonable assistance to Customer in responding to verified requests, taking into account the nature of processing and information available to GSM.

## 13. Audits and assurance

On reasonable written request and subject to confidentiality/security restrictions, GSM will provide information reasonably necessary for Customer to assess compliance with this DPA. The parties should use existing audit reports, security documentation and written responses before requiring intrusive on-site audits. Any audit must avoid access to other customers' or children's data and must not compromise platform security.

## 14. Return and deletion

At the end of the service, GSM will delete or return Customer Personal Data for which it acts solely as processor in accordance with the agreement and applicable law. Data may remain for a limited period in protected backups or where retention is required for safeguarding, fraud prevention, financial records, litigation hold or other legal obligation.

## 15. Liability

Liability under this DPA follows the liability allocation in the governing agreement, subject to rights and liabilities that Applicable Data Protection Law does not permit the parties to exclude or limit.

## 16. Jurisdiction-specific schedules

The parties may attach jurisdiction-specific schedules without rewriting the entire DPA, including:

- UK GDPR / Data Protection Act / international transfer schedule;
- EU GDPR / SCC schedule;
- US FERPA/COPPA schedule;
- US state student-privacy/privacy-law schedule; and
- other country schedules approved by counsel.
