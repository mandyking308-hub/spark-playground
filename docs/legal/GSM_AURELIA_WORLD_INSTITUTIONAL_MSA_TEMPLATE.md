# Aurelia World — Institutional Master Services Agreement template

**Supplier:** Global Solutions Management LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("GSM")  
**Product:** Aurelia World  
**Status:** Counsel-review draft — 22 August 2026  
**Use:** Schools, education groups and approved organisations. Do not execute until approved by qualified counsel and aligned to GSM's insurance, tax and operating capabilities.

---

## 1. Agreement structure

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA") governs the customer's institutional use of Aurelia World. Each signed order form identifies the customer, term, permitted users, service tier, fees, implementation scope and any agreed special terms.

Order of precedence unless an order expressly states otherwise:

1. mandatory law / applicable transfer clauses;
2. signed order form or negotiated special terms;
3. Data Processing Addendum (for processor matters);
4. this MSA;
5. referenced platform policies.

## 2. Licence and authorised use

Subject to payment and compliance with the agreement, GSM grants Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the subscription term for its authorised users to access the contracted Aurelia World services for the agreed educational, safeguarding, community or sponsored-programme purposes.

Customer may not resell, white-label, sublicense, scrape, reverse engineer, probe, circumvent security, extract data for unrelated purposes, or use the service to build a competing dataset/product except where a right cannot lawfully be restricted.

## 3. Customer administration and authorised users

Customer will:

- designate authorised administrators;
- maintain accurate role assignments;
- remove access promptly when a user no longer needs it;
- use reasonable steps to keep administrator credentials secure;
- provide only data it is authorised to provide;
- ensure staff comply with child-safety and acceptable-use requirements; and
- notify GSM promptly of suspected compromise, misuse or safeguarding concerns.

Institutional sponsorship does not create unrestricted access to children's private drafts or private workspaces.

## 4. Child safety and safeguarding

Customer and GSM will each comply with child-safety duties that apply directly to them.

Customer must not permit staff, sponsors, mentors or other adults to:

- contact a child outside approved Aurelia World pathways using platform-derived information;
- extract child contact/location details for unrelated use;
- use platform access for grooming, solicitation or commercial lead generation;
- bypass guardian permission, role, moderation or age-separation controls; or
- retaliate against a person for making a good-faith safety report.

GSM may immediately restrict an account, feature, opportunity, project or Customer integration where reasonably necessary to protect a child, preserve evidence, respond to a legal requirement or contain a serious security risk.

Nothing in the agreement requires GSM to disclose information where disclosure could prejudice a safeguarding, security, regulator or law-enforcement investigation.

## 5. Data protection

The parties will comply with the applicable Data Processing Addendum.

For institution-directed education records, GSM may act as processor/service provider on Customer's documented instructions. GSM may act as independent controller for account security, fraud prevention, child safeguarding, platform integrity, billing and its own legal compliance.

Customer must not instruct GSM to process health, biometric or other highly sensitive information unless the relevant feature and legal basis have been expressly approved in writing.

## 6. Security

GSM will maintain technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service and risk, which may include verified role-based access, database-level row security, server-side privileged credentials, audit trails, private-by-default child projects and protected permission workflows.

GSM may update security measures to respond to new threats or technology provided the overall level of protection is not materially reduced.

Customer is responsible for the security of its own devices, networks, identity systems and authorised-user administration.

## 7. Service changes and availability

GSM may improve, replace or discontinue features for security, legal, safety, technical or commercial reasons. GSM will not intentionally make a material reduction to the core paid service during a committed institutional term without providing a reasonable alternative, notice or other remedy where required by the agreement or law.

Unless a signed order includes a service-level commitment, no separate uptime service level is promised by this template.

## 8. Fees, invoicing and taxes

Customer will pay the fees in the order form according to the stated payment schedule.

Fees are exclusive of taxes unless the order says otherwise. Customer is responsible for legally applicable taxes other than taxes imposed on GSM's net income, subject to valid exemption documentation and applicable law.

Overdue undisputed amounts may be subject to lawful interest and, after reasonable notice, suspension. GSM will not suspend a child-safety reporting route merely as a debt-collection mechanism.

## 9. Intellectual property

GSM and its licensors retain all rights in Aurelia World, software, platform design, documentation, branding, databases, security architecture and other GSM materials except for Customer/Member Content.

Children and other content owners retain ownership of content they lawfully create. GSM receives only the operational licence described in the Terms/DPA and any additional licence expressly agreed for a specific institutional programme.

Customer grants GSM a limited right to use Customer names, marks and supplied materials solely to implement and operate the contracted service. Public case-study or marketing use requires separate approval unless the order form expressly permits it.

## 10. Feedback

If Customer voluntarily provides suggestions about the platform, GSM may use those suggestions without restriction or payment, provided GSM does not thereby obtain ownership of Customer confidential information, personal data or Customer-owned content.

## 11. Confidentiality

Each party receiving confidential information will:

- use it only for the agreement;
- protect it using reasonable care no less than the care used for its own similar information;
- disclose it only to personnel/advisers/providers who need it and are bound by appropriate confidentiality duties; and
- return/delete it when no longer required, subject to lawful retention.

Confidential information does not include information that is independently developed, lawfully received without restriction, becomes public without breach, or was already lawfully known.

A party may disclose confidential information where legally required, giving advance notice where legally permitted.

## 12. Warranties

Each party warrants that it has authority to enter into the agreement.

GSM warrants that it will provide the contracted service with reasonable skill and care and will not knowingly introduce malicious code into the service.

Except for express warranties and rights that cannot lawfully be excluded, GSM does not warrant that the service will be uninterrupted, error-free or suitable for every Customer purpose, or that user-generated content, third-party opportunities or AI-assisted outputs will always be accurate.

## 13. Compliance with law

Each party is responsible for laws applicable to its own conduct and role.

Customer will not use the service in violation of sanctions/export controls, anti-bribery rules, child-safety law, intellectual-property law or applicable education/privacy requirements.

GSM remains responsible for obligations that applicable law places directly on it as supplier, platform operator, controller or processor. Customer responsibilities do not transfer those obligations away from GSM.

## 14. Suspension

GSM may suspend affected access where reasonably necessary for:

- serious security risk;
- safeguarding risk;
- unlawful activity;
- material breach;
- persistent non-payment of undisputed fees after notice; or
- a binding legal/regulatory requirement.

Where practicable and safe, GSM will limit suspension to the affected user, feature or Customer area and provide notice/reason.

## 15. Term and renewal

The initial term and any renewal are stated in the order form. Automatic renewal will apply only where clearly agreed and lawful.

Institutional renewal terms should not be copied from consumer subscription flows without procurement/counsel approval.

## 16. Termination

Either party may terminate for a material breach not cured within the cure period stated in the order, or immediately where the breach cannot reasonably be cured or involves serious illegality/safeguarding risk.

Customer may terminate for convenience only if the order form grants that right.

On termination:

- Customer access ends on the effective date subject to safe transition arrangements;
- accrued fees remain payable;
- processor data is returned/deleted under the DPA;
- lawful safeguarding, security, financial and dispute records may be retained; and
- surviving provisions remain in effect.

## 17. Indemnities — counsel to finalise

### Customer indemnity

To the extent permitted by law, Customer will defend/indemnify GSM against third-party claims arising from Customer's unlawful instructions, unauthorised data disclosure, infringement by Customer-supplied materials, or material misuse of the service, except to the extent caused by GSM.

### GSM IP indemnity

Counsel should decide whether GSM will provide a negotiated IP infringement indemnity for the core unmodified platform, including standard exclusions for Customer content, combinations not supplied by GSM, Customer modifications/instructions and continued use after a replacement/workaround is provided.

**Do not sign institutional deals until this allocation is approved against insurance and customer expectations.**

## 18. Liability — counsel/insurance alignment required

Nothing excludes liability that law does not permit to be excluded or limited.

Subject to that rule, neither party should be liable for indirect, special, punitive or consequential losses or lost profits/revenue/goodwill, except for categories counsel expressly carves out.

Draft GSM aggregate cap: fees paid/payable under the affected order during the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.

**Counsel must set any enhanced/super-cap categories** (for example confidentiality, data protection, IP indemnity or wilful misconduct) in alignment with GSM's insurance and risk appetite. Do not accept unlimited institutional liability by default.

## 19. Insurance

GSM will maintain the insurance specified in the order form or otherwise agreed. Counsel/operations should confirm available limits for cyber/privacy, technology E&O, media/IP and any relevant safeguarding exposure before promising coverage levels in procurement questionnaires.

## 20. Publicity

Neither party may issue a press release naming the other without approval. Customer logo/case-study use requires written permission unless expressly granted in the order form.

## 21. Assignment and corporate transactions

GSM may assign the agreement to an affiliate or successor in connection with financing, reorganisation, merger, acquisition or sale of substantially all relevant assets, subject to applicable privacy law.

Customer may assign only with GSM's consent, not unreasonably withheld in a bona fide reorganisation, unless the order says otherwise.

## 22. Governing law and disputes

Draft B2B position: Delaware law and an agreed Delaware forum, unless the order form states another negotiated jurisdiction.

Counsel should consider whether arbitration, court jurisdiction, escalation/mediation or local public-sector procurement rules are more appropriate for target institutional customers.

## 23. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events outside reasonable control, excluding payment obligations already due. The affected party will use reasonable efforts to mitigate and resume performance.

## 24. General

The agreement is the entire agreement for its subject matter and replaces prior proposals on that subject. Amendments must be in writing by authorised representatives. Failure to enforce a right is not a waiver. Unenforceable provisions are severed/reformed to the minimum extent required. Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted where lawful.
